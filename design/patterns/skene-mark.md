## Constraints

From `machine/components.yaml` (`patterns.skeneMark`).

| prop | allowed |
| --- | --- |
| `tone` | `block`, `onDark`, `onLight` |
| `size` | px, square |
| `radius` | px, applied only to `block` — the other two have no fill to round |

- tone names the GROUND, not the ink: onLight is the BLACK glyph. Naming it after the ink is what makes a caller pick the invisible variant.
- block — the peach glyph on its own black tile — is the default because it brings its own ground and is safe anywhere. There is no peach-on-transparent file; do not fake one with a mask recolour, the counters are part of the artwork.
- Anywhere the product speaks for itself — the featured Bridge card, the AgentCallout avatar — this is the mark. Do not draw a ring glyph or a letter "S" as a stand-in.
- SkeneLockup is the symbol WITH the wordmark, and it is sized by `height` rather than by `size`: the artwork is 1016x260 and one number meaning width in one component and height in the other ships at the wrong scale. Its `accent` tone is the only one not named after a ground — peach symbol, white wordmark, dark grounds only.
- SkeneLockup defaults alt="Skene". A nav bar has no word beside it, so the lockup usually IS the accessible name; the mark defaults the other way because it sits next to type that already says it.

## Rules that are not negotiable

1. **`chrome.*` is invariant and cannot invert.** `color.chrome.surface.*` and
   `color.chrome.text.*` are always dark. Use them only on a surface that never
   flips — a terminal, a log panel, a code frame. Anything on a surface that
   flips uses the theme-aware `color.text.*` / `color.surface.*`. The two share
   their dark values and diverge only in light, so the wrong pick looks correct
   until someone opens light mode.
2. **A light surface on a dark page needs the `light` class on its own root.**
   Without it the mode-aware tokens resolve to their dark values against a light
   fill. That has shipped text at 1.08:1. Check the module's `polarity` first —
   `applies-light` means the module already does this for you.
3. **Content is props.** No section hardcodes copy.

Theming is class-based and the package ships base mode `dark`. Contrast floors: body text 4.5:1, large text 3:1 (WCAG 2.2).
