## Constraints

From `machine/components.yaml` (`patterns.marketing`).

- Eyebrow is the section-heading kicker and owns font.tracking.eyebrow (0.16em). It is NOT interchangeable with Badge, which is the product surface's status primitive, nor with StatChip/MetaChip.
- PillNav sits OVER the hero rather than above it, so media runs to the top of the viewport. The translucent fill plus blur is what keeps links legible over arbitrary photography without a solid bar.
- Eyebrow on a cream panel takes `onLight`. Its default border and ink are invariant chrome.* and do NOT follow a `light` ancestor, so on cream the chip keeps its dark-page colours. Do not write the two-utility className override that predates the prop; three modules here and fourteen call sites on the marketing site did, which is what the prop replaced.
- Anything in the pill bar that is not a plain anchor composes through PillNavLink `asChild` — a menu trigger, a next/link, a button. Copying the bar's seven-utility class string is what puts its hover ink, its active peach and its 4px radius in two places. `href` is not forwarded under `asChild`, and an item with no `href` does not appear in the mobile drawer; pass one when it should.

## What binds this module

- **Polarity `inherits`.** It puts no theme class on its own root, so it takes the page. Place it on a light fill and the `light` class is yours to add, or its mode-aware tokens resolve to their DARK values against that fill — measured at 1.07:1 on rendered pixels.
- **`chrome.*` is invariant.** This module is in the fixed-dark world, so the distinction bites here: `chrome.*` never inverts and belongs only on a surface that never flips. Anything on a surface that follows the theme uses `text.*` / `surface.*`. The two share their dark values, so the wrong pick looks correct until someone opens light mode.
- **Content is props.** This pattern hardcodes no copy; the page supplies it.

These are the rules this module can break. The full set, the surface roles and
the contrast floors are in [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).
