- status binds the reserved vocabulary to semantic.matcha / warningAmber / errorRed, the same three Finding uses. Never peach: peach is the primary action colour and a peach fault reads as a call to action. The captured ring's gold is the demo's own accent fork.
- The default is warn, not good. The only shipped instance reads "coverage needs attention" at 72, so a forgotten prop must not render a failing score in the reassurance colour.
- Theme-aware: digits take text.*, and the track is mixed from currentColor so it follows a `light` ancestor. There is no onLight prop and there should not be one.
