## Use for

The "ask us anything" prompt block: an attributed question rendered as the loud heading, a quiet borderless textarea that the heading labels via `aria-labelledby`, an AI-disclosure badge, and a small submit sized to its label in the bottom-right. It is the package's one genuinely interactive section — a controlled textarea plus a submit handler — and it lives in its own module specifically so `'use client'` does not spread to server-renderable siblings. The restraint is the design: it is a low-commitment probe, so a full-bleed primary button and a boxed input would make it read as a form rather than as a question.

## Constraints

From `machine/components.yaml` (`sections.AskWidget`).

- Client component — controlled textarea. It is the only interactive part of its section and lives in its own module for that reason.
- The submit button is bottom-right of the textarea, not full width. It is a low-commitment probe and a full-width primary button oversells it.
- showAiBadge defaults true. The answer is model-generated and the badge is the disclosure; keep it minor, not optional-by-default.

## What binds this module

- **Polarity `inherits`.** It puts no theme class on its own root, so it takes the page. Place it on a light fill and the `light` class is yours to add, or its mode-aware tokens resolve to their DARK values against that fill — measured at 1.07:1 on rendered pixels.
- **`chrome.*` is invariant.** This module is in the fixed-dark world, so the distinction bites here: `chrome.*` never inverts and belongs only on a surface that never flips. Anything on a surface that follows the theme uses `text.*` / `surface.*`. The two share their dark values, so the wrong pick looks correct until someone opens light mode.
- **Content is props.** This section hardcodes no copy; the page supplies it.
