## Use for

The monthly/yearly switch, and the only `'use client'` file in the pricing section. It exists as its own module for a structural reason rather than a visual one: putting the directive in `plan-card.tsx` would drag the whole pure-markup card across the client boundary, so the interactivity was quarantined here (sections.md rule 4). It is fully controlled — `yearly` in, `onChange` out — and makes both word labels clickable, not just the 40px track, because the words are the obvious target.
