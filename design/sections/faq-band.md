## Constraints

From `machine/components.yaml` (`sections.FaqBand`).

| prop | allowed |
| --- | --- |
| `multiple` | open many rows at once; default is one at a time, collapsible |
| `note` | the line under the heading, usually a talk-to-us link |

- Composes @radix-ui/react-accordion directly rather than ui/accordion. The primitive bakes a ChevronDown into its trigger and this band's mark is a round +/× at the right edge; wrapping it would mean rendering a chevron and hiding it.
- The whole module is 'use client' because disclosure is state. FaqRow needs useId for a stable Radix item value — a slug of the question text would collide and a counter would not survive reordering.
- `light` on the root, never conditional. The band ships in one tone; a tone="dark" prop would have to flip that class and no FAQ on either surface is the dark one.
