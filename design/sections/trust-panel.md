## Use for

A cream panel split into a claim and its evidence: heading, lede and a row of links on the left, a stack of `TrustFact` items on a deeper cream to the right. The split is the argument — the reassurance is not a paragraph asserting itself but a paragraph standing next to checkable facts, which is why the evidence track is the wider of the two (`0.9fr 1.1fr`). It is a fixed-polarity object: the root carries `light` unconditionally, so it is a cream card on a dark page, with a peach bloom rising from the bottom-left corner that the token system gets right on cream for free.

## Watch for

| note | via |
| --- | --- |
| `light` is unconditional and first in the class list. There is no dark variant and you cannot make one by overriding `className` — `bg-brand-light` is an invariant cream, and every mode-aware token in the subtree has already resolved for a cream ground. | `className` |
| `TrustFact`'s hairlines and its icon circle's border are `chrome-line-on-light` — an invariant dark rule at 14% designed for cream. Lifted onto a dark surface those rules and that circle are effectively invisible. The doc comment calls this out as the one deliberate exception to the theme-aware-tokens rule. Since 0.11.0 that is what `TrustFact`'s `tone="muted"` exists for — the theme-following pair for any other ground. | `export TrustFact` |
| `children` renders directly inside the `<ul>` (chosen over an inner wrapper specifically to avoid `display: contents`, which several browsers still strip list semantics from). Pass `TrustFact`s, i.e. `<li>`s — a `<div>` or a wrapper component breaks the "3 of 5" a screen reader would otherwise give. | `prop TrustPanel.children` |
| The fact separators live on the item (`border-b … first:pt-0 last:border-b-0 last:pb-0`), not on a divider element, so the stack must not also be spaced by a parent `gap` — that double-spaces the rhythm. The icon circle also renders when `icon` is absent, on purpose, to keep every title's left edge aligned. | `prop TrustFact.icon` |
| `title` is capped at `max-w-[440px]` and `lede` at `max-w-[430px]`, and the glow is absolutely positioned inside the left cell only. Long copy will not fill the column, and the bloom never reaches the facts panel. | `prop TrustPanel.title` |

## What binds this module

- **Polarity `applies-light`.** It puts `light` on its own root and brings its own ground. You do NOT owe it the light class, and adding one around it is redundant at best.
- **`chrome.*` is invariant.** This module is in the fixed-dark world, so the distinction bites here: `chrome.*` never inverts and belongs only on a surface that never flips. Anything on a surface that follows the theme uses `text.*` / `surface.*`. The two share their dark values, so the wrong pick looks correct until someone opens light mode.
- **Content is props.** This section hardcodes no copy; the page supplies it.

These are the rules this module can break. The full set, the surface roles and
the contrast floors are in [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).
