## Constraints

- It takes NO PROPS. Every string, every table name and every step label is hardcoded, and the set is the pre-swap onboarding vocabulary (onboarding_started, public.accounts, Signed up / Onboarding started / Reached first value). A consumer that needs different content forks the file; one has. Do not add a call-site workaround, and do not assume a content prop exists because every other section has one.
- The stage is LIGHT and deliberately so — --color-background-darker is #ffffff, and softPulse is ink rather than peach because peach measures ~1.2:1 on cream. It was built for a `Bridge` band. On a near-black hero it is the wrong reading and there is no tone prop to say so.
- Four geometry defects ship at 0.12.0 and are recorded in documentation/20260825_journey_signal_scene_design.md under "Known defects at 0.12.0": WIDE_MIN 720 sends 768px viewports to the desktop layout at 0.66 scale, MEDIUM.left.w 170 ellipsises both Evidence rows, MEDIUM.h 640 leaves ~5px under the PR panel, and MEDIUM reserves no top strip for an overlaid control. Read that section before changing any number in the three Layout constants.
- Nothing in this repository consumed MEDIUM before the one external consumer did, which is how all four shipped. A story that renders the scene at a few widths is not a check on a hand-placed layout; measure the panels at the width a hero column actually gives it.
