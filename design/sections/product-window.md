## Use for

The chrome that frames a product mock inside a marketing section: rounded border, modal shadow, and an optional title bar with a left title and a right `status` slot. Its default `tone="light"` is the argument rather than a style choice — the page around it is Skene's dark chrome, so the thing inside the frame reads as the customer's own tool; a dark window on a dark page reads as a screenshot of Skene itself. Ships alongside `WindowStatus` (the title bar's chip), `WindowToolbar` (a filter/breadcrumb strip) and `WindowChip`.

## Watch for

| note | via |
| --- | --- |
| `tone="light"` puts the `light` class on the root and that is load-bearing, not decoration: without it every mode-aware token inside — `semantic.matcha`, `errorRed`, `brand.peach` — resolves to its DARK value against the cream fill (matcha #d7f4ab on #faf1e9 is 1.16:1). | `WindowToolbar` is a dark-window part despite living in this file: it hardcodes `border-chrome-line-subtle` and `text-chrome-text-muted-warm`, both invariant dark-surface tokens, so inside a light window the rule is near-invisible and the text colour is wrong. | `max-w-[640px]` is a class and can be overridden through `className` (cn is twMerge), but `boxShadow: var(--shadow-modal)` is an inline style and no class will beat it. | `inline style` |

## Constraints

From `machine/components.yaml` (`sections.ProductWindow`).

| --- | --- |
| `tone` | `light`, `dark` |

- WindowStatus is a Chip with a narrower vocabulary (healthy | live) and a 0.05em tracking override. Style changes to the chip shape belong in sections/chip.tsx, not here.
- tone=light is the DEFAULT and means "the customer's product". A dark window on a dark page reads as a screenshot of Skene itself.
- tone=light applies the `light` class. This is load-bearing, not decoration: mode-aware tokens inside otherwise resolve to their DARK value against a cream fill (semantic.matcha #d7f4ab on #faf1e9 is 1.16:1, and the status pill was illegible). Do not remove it.
- Internal rules use chrome.line.onLight, not chrome.line.subtle.

## What binds this module

- **Polarity `applies-light`.** It puts `light` on its own root and brings its own ground. You do NOT owe it the light class, and adding one around it is redundant at best.
- **`chrome.*` is invariant.** This module is in the fixed-dark world, so the distinction bites here: `chrome.*` never inverts and belongs only on a surface that never flips. Anything on a surface that follows the theme uses `text.*` / `surface.*`. The two share their dark values, so the wrong pick looks correct until someone opens light mode.
- **Content is props.** This section hardcodes no copy; the page supplies it.

These are the rules this module can break. The full set, the surface roles and
the contrast floors are in [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).
