## Not for — reach for this instead

| instead | why |

| `ValueCard` | Cards that argue by contrast — N of them naming what the situation costs, then one naming what it becomes. These cards are peers; the tone here marks the lit one rather than turning the argument. |
| `FeatureRow` | A whole band: a copy column beside a visual panel, at band scale. Its copy column alone carries 38.4px of side padding, wider than the content a 223px cell holds. This grid is what goes IN a FeatureRow's visual, not a nest of four more. |

## Watch for

| note | via |
| --- | --- |
| The featured card carries `light` and the other cards carry `dark`, and both are load-bearing. Without them a mode-aware token keeps the wrong value — `text.primary` resolves to #faf1e9 on a #faf1e9 fill, which is not dim, it is absent. | `prop SurfaceCards.featured` |
| The chip reads as tested, which is exactly why it is optional. Pass one only where the exact string is verifiable in a source; an absent chip is not a hole in the design. | `type SurfaceCardItem.code` |
| The glyph is a character, never a named HTML entity. JSX entity tables are per-compiler and `machine/rules.yaml` bans them outright. | `type SurfaceCardItem.icon` |
| The grid is literally two across at `sm` and one below it, not `auto-fit`. Four tracks in a ~640px band give each card 139px, and every two-word title wraps. | `prop SurfaceCards.surfaces` |
