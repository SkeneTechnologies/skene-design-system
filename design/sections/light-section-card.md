## Use for

The inverted band: one cream card carrying a whole section's worth of copy — heading, italic promise, a ruled proof block, actions — with an optional visual panel opposite. It is `FeatureRow` at the other polarity, and its `light` root is a load-bearing accessibility mechanism, which is why the two were deliberately NOT collapsed behind a `tone` flag. SCOPE NARROWED IN 0.9.19: this is no longer a marketing section band. `render_marketing_cards_as_feature_row` gives that role to `FeatureRow` alone, because a page that alternates one repeating object cannot also alternate polarity without the cream band reading as a second system. What remains is every non-marketing use — product surfaces, gallery cases, and any dark-document context needing a correctly-inverted cream subtree, which is the part no other component does.

## Watch for

| note | via |
| --- | --- |
| `light` on the root is unconditional and first in the class list. Anything dark you nest has to re-assert `dark` itself: the gallery writes `<ProductWindow tone="dark" className="dark">`, because ProductWindow's dark branch sets `chrome.*` fills but adds no `dark` class of its own. | `className` |
| `brand.peach` inverts inside this card — #89684a, not #fec089 — so a default peach `<Button>` lands noticeably darker than the same button on the page around it. - Root carries the `light` class and it is load-bearing. This is a cream surface on a dark page; without it every mode-aware token inside resolves to its dark value against the fill and the text disappears. Same mechanism as ProductWindow tone=light and the featured PlanCard.
- The COPY column gets the wider track, in both modes: 1.1fr/0.9fr, and `reverse` is 0.9fr/1.1fr, which only mirrors sides. There is no ratio prop. Every cream copy-and-visual band on skene-marketing-website gives the wider track to the VISUAL, at 5fr/7fr, so all seven of them stay hand-rolled. Overriding the tracks with a className from the call site is precisely what page-template.tsx deleted TONAL_GRID to stop.
- It splits at `md` (768), hardcoded. FeatureRow has `splitAt` for this; this does not. Every band on the marketing site splits at `lg` (1024).
- The two above are unresolved upstream asks, not preferences, and they are why context.yaml's routing and the feature_row_as_the_marketing_card documentation disagree about whether this is the cream copy-and-buttons panel at all. Until they are settled, a cream band with copy and a visual is hand-rolled from the recipe in layouts.yaml `marketing.cream_inset`.

## What binds this module

- **Polarity `applies-light`.** It puts `light` on its own root and brings its own ground. You do NOT owe it the light class, and adding one around it is redundant at best.
- **`chrome.*` is invariant.** This module is in the fixed-dark world, so the distinction bites here: `chrome.*` never inverts and belongs only on a surface that never flips. Anything on a surface that follows the theme uses `text.*` / `surface.*`. The two share their dark values, so the wrong pick looks correct until someone opens light mode.
- **Content is props.** This section hardcodes no copy; the page supplies it.

These are the rules this module can break. The full set, the surface roles and
the contrast floors are in [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).
