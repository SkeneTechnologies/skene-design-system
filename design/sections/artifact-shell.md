## Constraints

From `machine/components.yaml` (`sections.ArtifactShell`).

| prop | allowed |
| --- | --- |
| `ArtFrame.kind` | `gh`, `db`, `jr` |
| `ArtFrame.row` | boolean — the frame sits in a row rather than a column |
| `StatPill.status` | `good`, `warn`, `bad`, `neutral` |
| `DataCell.mono` | boolean |
| `DataCell.muted` | boolean |

- `kind` on ArtFrame is REQUIRED and the pairing is not decoration: gh is GitHub, PRs and editor chrome; db is schema, connections and keys; jr is journeys, funnels and measurement. A schema panel on the GitHub texture is not a styling slip, it is a miscue. Same three assets as SectionBackdrop, under different names — journey/github/schema there, jr/gh/db here. That divergence is real and unreconciled; do not "fix" it at a call site by passing the other vocabulary.
- The two registers are a decision, not a default. ArtPanel is fixed dark because it depicts GitHub, an editor or a shell, and drawing those light is a lie; its tokens are terminal.* and terminal-chrome.*, invariant by construction. AppWindow carries `light` unconditionally because the real signed-in product IS light (bg-card appears 127 times under app/workspace/**) and these artifacts render on a dark page. Do not remove either: `cn` is twMerge and it cannot unset a theme class, so an artifact that inherits the page's polarity is a picture of a product that does not exist. A consumer needing AppWindow to follow the ambient theme wants a prop and a decision, not a className.
- Every padding in this module is a literal px ported from a --spacing-N token, NOT the numerically-similar Tailwind step. The package sets --spacing: 0.2rem, so p-4 is 12.8px against --spacing-4's 16px. They differ by 25% and nothing warns. Radii do line up: --radius-gtm is --radius-xl is rounded-xl (14px). Tidying a px value onto the numeric scale is the single most repeated defect in this package — logo-row.tsx shipped at 80% of its documented size that way.
- StatPill derives every edge and fill from the one status colour, so a caller cannot produce a red pill with an amber rim. Its dot is a real aria-hidden span rather than a ::before, deliberately: as a pseudo-element it reads as a glyph pixel to the contrast harness and skews the measurement of every artifact containing a pill.
- AppWindow drops its bar whole when there is neither crumb nor actions. That is not a convenience — the funnel artifact is deliberately unbranded, because no Skene surface renders a funnel and the chart is the reader's own dashboard. Do not add chrome to give it a header.
- DataTable takes rows as CHILDREN because its cells are not uniform down a column — an event name is mono, a location mono and quiet, a status a StatPill. If your cells ARE uniform down a column, you have a reference table: use KeyValueTable, which takes rows as data for exactly that reason.

## What binds this module

- **Polarity `applies-light`.** It puts `light` on its own root and brings its own ground. You do NOT owe it the light class, and adding one around it is redundant at best.
- **`chrome.*` is invariant.** This module is in the fixed-dark world, so the distinction bites here: `chrome.*` never inverts and belongs only on a surface that never flips. Anything on a surface that follows the theme uses `text.*` / `surface.*`. The two share their dark values, so the wrong pick looks correct until someone opens light mode.
- **Content is props.** This section hardcodes no copy; the page supplies it.

These are the rules this module can break. The full set, the surface roles and
the contrast floors are in [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).
